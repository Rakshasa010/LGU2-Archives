ORDINANCE NO. 2026-001

SECTION 1. Title. This ordinance shall be known as the "Local Water Safety Ordinance of 2026."

SECTION 2. Coverage. This ordinance applies to all public swimming pools and commercial beach resorts within the city.

SECTION 3. Safety Requirements. All establishments shall provide at least one certified lifeguard on duty during operating hours. Lifebuoys must be positioned at intervals of not more than fifty (50) meters along the shoreline.

SECTION 4. Effectivity. This ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in a newspaper of general circulation.

Approved by the Sangguniang Panlungsod this 10th day of June 2026.
