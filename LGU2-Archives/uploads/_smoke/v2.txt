ORDINANCE NO. 2026-001

SECTION 1. Title. This ordinance shall be known as the "Local Water Safety Ordinance of 2026."

SECTION 2. Coverage. This ordinance applies to all public swimming pools, commercial beach resorts, and private resort facilities within the city.

SECTION 3. Safety Requirements. All establishments shall provide at least two (2) certified lifeguards on duty during operating hours. Lifebuoys must be positioned at intervals of not more than thirty (30) meters along the shoreline. Diving boards higher than three (3) meters shall require a separate safety inspection.

SECTION 4. Penalties. Any violation of this ordinance shall be punishable by a fine of not less than Five Thousand Pesos (PHP 5,000.00).

SECTION 5. Effectivity. This ordinance shall take effect thirty (30) days after its publication in a newspaper of general circulation.

Approved by the Sangguniang Panlungsod this 25th day of July 2026.
