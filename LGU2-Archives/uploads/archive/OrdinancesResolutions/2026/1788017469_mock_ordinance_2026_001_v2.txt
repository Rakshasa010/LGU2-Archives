ORDINANCE NO. 2026-001 (AMENDED)

AN ORDINANCE ADOPTING THE ANNUAL BUDGET OF THE SANGGUNIANG BAYAN
OF VALENZUELA CITY FOR THE FISCAL YEAR 2026, AS AMENDED.

Be it enacted by the Sangguniang Bayan of Valenzuela City:

SECTION 1. The Annual Budget for FY 2026 is hereby adopted in the
total amount of Php 55,000,000.00 (increased from Php 50,000,000.00).

SECTION 2. The increase shall be allocated to infrastructure projects.

SECTION 3. This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.

Approved: February 20, 2026
Author: Hon. Juan Dela Cruz
Reference: ORD-2026-001
Note: This is an updated version with increased budget allocation.
